The spa floors of the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]]. You enter through double doors into a warm, heavy, damp warren of corridors and treatment rooms. It is far bigger than any weekend spa: there are hundreds of rooms, dozens of staff are constantly coming and going, and most of the staff are Gungans. Guests wander about in towels.

## The Water

According to the staff induction, the water is the whole point. It comes from a mineral-rich geothermal aquifer tapped off the mountain and channelled through the floor in warm streams. In the passages it runs in open channels beside seating. In the treatment rooms it passes beneath the walls into basins, small cascades and ledges, then out through the far side. The channels are about as wide as a book, but the current at the bottom is strong enough to carry a Gand from one room to a passage elsewhere.

A schematic left on a front-desk clipboard shows that the rooms are connected by these waterways and by back entrances. 6 Delta is close to 6 Charlie and 5 Delta.

## Treatment Room 6 Delta

The room booked for [[ilyra-dane]] and [[casimir-aldovane]]'s 12:30 appointment. It has two beds laid head to head, bowls and implements for using the water, and a curtained second door at the back. Both doors are controlled by keypads.

In Session VIII, someone locked both doors from outside, disabled the room's controls and drove the temperature up until the walls were hot to touch. [[kess]] got the crew out by slicing through the panel using [[ottho]]'s security keys while somebody else worked against him inside the system.
