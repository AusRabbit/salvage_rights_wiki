Small, ugly, uniquely beautiful depending on who is describing it, and currently carrying most of a desert in its ducting. None of the buttons are labelled and none of them do what you would expect.

Aboard her, [[kess]]'s social checks are upgraded — a mechanical quirk the crew leaned on twice this session. She also has weapons that fold out of the hull on command, which ended a fight before it began. The hyperspace generator is described as tenuous, though that may have been part of a lie.

Currently parked in an auxiliary berth in the [[servants-quarters]] of the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]], too cramped for repairs or unloading, with three prisoners, three ex-Imperials, and a stowaway aboard.
