One of [[vessaly]]'s attendants. The crew's attempts to calm him in the galley of the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] only made him cry harder. Vessaly ordered him to remain aboard and watch the unconscious [[michael]] while the crew entered the resort.

Sean warned that the missing staff would eventually be noticed. He did not prevent Michael from waking and leaving the ship.
