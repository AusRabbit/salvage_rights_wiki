Uniformed security staff of the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]], answering to [[severin]]. [[michael]] and [[trent]] are two of them. They wear the same uniforms the crew have used as cover.

## The Encounter

Two attendants followed the [[skylark-security-drone|Skylark drones]] to [[iresa-tarn-kavu]]'s suite and could not keep up with them.

- [[rethven]]'s abandoned room-service trolley blocked the corridor. They complained loudly and shoved it aside.
- They ordered a drone sent “in through the chute” when the door would not open.
- They threw their weight against the door again and again. Rethven held it with leverage and technique rather than strength until they tore it off its hinges.
- [[carsk]]'s stun covering fire drove them back from the doorway long enough for the crew to reach the balcony.
- They forced the door in just as the crew went over the side.

The crew drew no organic blood, but a blaster has been fired inside the Splendor and security will not let that go.

## What the Crew Know

- The resort has smoke alarms but **no blaster detection**. Security relies on panic buttons, drones and people.
- They respond fast, and send the drones ahead of the guards.
- Their record with the resort's Gungan staff is ugly; see Trent.
