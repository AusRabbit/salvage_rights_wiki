A former Republic judicial archivist who now operates a sealed-evidence vault in an old storm-drain exchange beneath the Rain Market on [[port-heska]]. The entrance is an unmarked blue-black door with three vertical scratches near its frame. [[un-momento]] sent the crew to her because Marda could establish custody, hold material in escrow and arrange evidence releases that would make corporate erasure expensive.

Marda accepted [[kess]] and [[sel]] into a secured intake after Kess gave an honest account of the [[cygnus-biotech]] operation, the surviving witnesses and the anomaly associated with the [[specimen]]. Her working rule was simple: “Facts first. Identities when they become material.”

Marda authenticated and timestamped a recording derived from [[rhett]]'s evidence, creating a sealed copy outside Cygnus control. A corporate verification process stirred during authentication before she cut the connection.

She wanted the physical sample to establish a stronger chain of custody, but the crew later sold it to [[ilyra-dane]]. She gave Kess a proof-of-intake data wafer. Her sealed recording remains a surviving evidence trail and a potentially awkward promise.
