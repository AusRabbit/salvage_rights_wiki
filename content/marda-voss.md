Accepted [[kess]] and [[sel]] into a secured evidence intake at [[port-heska]]. Kess provided an honest account of the [[cygnus-biotech]] operation, the surviving witnesses and the anomaly associated with the [[specimen]].

Marda authenticated and timestamped a recording derived from [[rhett]]'s evidence, creating a sealed copy outside Cygnus control. A corporate verification process stirred during authentication before she cut the connection.

She wanted the physical sample to establish a stronger chain of custody, but the crew later sold it to [[ilyra-dane]]. Her sealed recording remains a surviving evidence trail and a potentially awkward promise.
