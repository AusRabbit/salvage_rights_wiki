> [!note] Reconstruction boundary
> This history comes from evolving campaign ledgers and a partial narrative summary. It preserves only the plot spine that remained consistent across those records. Scene details, exact sequencing and provisional material have been omitted rather than allowed to compete with the recorded Sessions V and VI.

## The Harbinger's Due

The [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] answered a distress signal from the drifting freighter [[harbingers-due|CSS Harbinger's Due]] in the Gorrath asteroid belt. [[kess]] and [[carsk]] boarded while a rival salvage crew led by [[verrin-kallax]] and a corporate recovery force closed on the same prize.

The derelict held an escaped organism capable of moving through conductive structures and interfering with ship systems. Cold and compartment isolation could hinder it, but not make it harmless. The salvage operation became a running fight through the ship.

The crew escaped with the wounded engineer [[rhett]], a severed piece of the organism, cryogenic equipment and evidence that [[cygnus-biotech]] had come to recover a specific target rather than rescue the missing crew. The organism later became known to them as [[specimen|MYRMEX-9]].

## Port Heska

The survivors and evidence made the crew valuable to several competing interests. At [[port-heska]], Kallax's local people attempted to gain control of the situation while the crew sought a safer chain of custody.

Carsk's former contact [[un-momento]] identified the people searching for the ship and sent the crew to [[marda-voss]]. The confrontation that followed left [[pavo]] dead, [[drenn]] incapacitated and [[talla-breen]] coerced into releasing the ship. The violence remained on service surveillance.

Marda accepted and sealed an anomaly recording made from Rhett's evidence, although the crew did not surrender the physical sample to her. Cygnus may have learned that specimen data had been authenticated.

## The Long Wake

The crew arranged to sell the severed limb and cryogenic equipment aboard [[long-wake|the Long Wake]], a moving scrap barge over Ord Mantell. The buyer was [[ilyra-dane]], who identified the organism as MYRMEX-9 and connected it to an older Imperial quarantine programme and an Outer Rim origin site.

Dane paid the crew, supplied cover for the Wayfarer's Debt and provided the coordinates that kept the mystery alive. Her people helped cover the crew's departure when an unidentified vessel approached under a Port Heska transponder shell.

## Back to the Origin

The crew later collected Dane's former quarantine team at [[wreckfall-nine]] and returned to the origin world. The expedition established that the Imperial response had sealed the site without understanding it, and that the entity could imitate voices, use accurate personal knowledge and sometimes issue truthful warnings.

Evidence at a second structure indicated that unknown visitors had returned years after the Imperial withdrawal and left a recorder as a deliberate marker. The expedition into [[the-dome]] ultimately led to the bargain that drives the present campaign: recover the specimen's missing components from Dane's network in exchange for access beneath the site.
