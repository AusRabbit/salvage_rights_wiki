Met the ship at the ramp with a data pad and a list of forms, and held the line on paperwork right up until the ship's guns came out. Cried, but less than his assistants, and was identified by [[kess]] as the bravest of them on exactly that basis.

What turned him was not fear. It was the suggestion of an undeclared, very valuable cargo for a very particular client. He started licking his lips, forgot he was a prisoner, and negotiated himself from ten percent up to thirty. He has the run of the facility, a data pad full of its secrets, and a torn uniform that [[kess]] offered to mend.

He is an asset for exactly as long as the arrangement is the best one available to him.
