Met the ship at the ramp with a data pad and a list of forms, and held the line on paperwork right up until the ship's guns came out. Cried, but less than his assistants, and was identified by [[kess]] as the bravest of them on exactly that basis.

What turned him was not fear. It was the suggestion of an undeclared, very valuable cargo for a very particular client. He started licking his lips, forgot he was a prisoner, and negotiated himself from ten percent to thirty, then to fifty as he led the crew into the resort.

Vessaly installed them in an eighth-floor penthouse, warned them that [[severin]] watches guest searches, and introduced them to [[ottho]]. When his associate failed to contain [[michael]], he surrendered five percent for the error. His current cut is forty-five percent.

He is an asset for exactly as long as the arrangement is the best one available to him.
