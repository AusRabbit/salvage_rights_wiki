Sent into [[the-dome]] alongside the crew with a second set of orders: no loose ends. They lost [[vossk]] and never beat the [[specimen]]. What is left — [[brak-tanris]], [[tavi-renn]] and [[emon-vale]] — kept their Imperial discipline and their hand signals right up until the whiskey, and have now thrown in with the [[wayfarers-debt]] for as long as it suits them.

At the Splendor they stayed aboard the ship, eating the crew's food, complaining about the sand and giving the crew the cold shoulder. They want no part in going after Dane, and want to be dropped somewhere.
