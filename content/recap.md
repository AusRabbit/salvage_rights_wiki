The crew lifted off the dome planet with sand in every vent of the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] and a passenger list they had not planned on. Somewhere in the ducting, [[rhett]] was keeping his head down and working off what he owed for putting the ship in the dirt.

## Sand in the Vents

Hyperspace did what hyperspace does: wounds closed, tempers cooled, and a plate of [[kess]]'s dinner was buried under a cough of grit from the air vent. The one injury that would not sleep off was his, and he could hardly stitch himself. [[rethven]] took the needle. [[sel]] offered to assist, and [[kess]] had to decide whether the clicking she had been making in the corridors at night was enough to keep her hands off him.

He let her help. It went better than anyone expected — [[sel]] turned out to be a deep well of medical knowledge, which is either something she had always hidden or something that has recently become true of her.

## Sixteen Whiskies

Three survivors of [[vossk-squad]] were still aboard, huddled at the dining table with nowhere to be: [[brak-tanris]], who had inherited command he never asked for, and his two, [[tavi-renn]] and [[emon-vale]]. [[carsk]] went in on shared service history and a hand on the shoulder, and read the room well enough to catch [[tavi-renn]]'s hand sliding under the table and [[brak-tanris]]'s single, silent shake of the head.

[[kess]] defused it with hospitality — a percussive slap to the sand-jammed drinks machine produced a tray of [[corellian-whiskey]], and the tension went out of the room. What followed was, by any measure, the most productive drinking session the crew has had: sixteen whiskies deep, [[carsk]] was standing on the table leading a chorus, and the last of the [[vossk-squad]] wariness dissolved into something that looked a lot like loyalty.

The admission, when it came, was blunt. [[brak-tanris]]'s people had been sent along with orders that the crew were not supposed to come back. Whatever [[brak-tanris]] owes them now, he owes the [[wayfarers-debt]] more.

## The Vain Commander

Asked how one gets to [[ilyra-dane]], [[brak-tanris]] did not describe a fortress. He described a weakness. Every few cycles the commander slips off the books and goes to [[naboo]], to the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]] — a mountainside retreat of engineered waterfalls and domed roofs where the galaxy's elite buy their youth back a treatment at a time. [[brak-tanris]] knows the retreat, and he knows when she is due.

The [[specimen]]'s missing components are somewhere behind [[ilyra-dane]], in a facility [[brak-tanris]] says the seven of them could not crack. She herself, half-dressed in a spa robe, is another matter.

## Sixteen Tons of Exfoliating Sand

The crew came in at the service entrance under the only cover story a ship full of grit could support: a bulk delivery of exfoliating sand. Traffic control was unconvinced, had no berth, no manifest and no patience, and held them in orbit for hours while the crew burned nerves in circles. Eventually the [[wayfarers-debt]] was allowed down onto a cramped auxiliary pad in the [[servants-quarters]], where no cargo could be unloaded and no repairs made.

Waiting at the bottom of the ramp was [[vessaly]], a bureaucrat with a data pad, two assistants and a security officer, all wearing the same expression at the sandy freighter now parked in their auxiliary port. He wanted forms the crew did not have, and answers about sixteen tons of cargo that plainly would not fit.

## It Goes Wrong at the Ramp

[[carsk]] lured one of the assistants aboard, put him against a bulkhead and made it clear that the crew needed uniforms. He may have overdone it — the young man went back down the ramp barely holding himself together. [[rethven]], deciding that nobody was looking at him, made a break for the door and did not get there before the [[security-officer]] did.

[[kess]] ended the fight before it started: a few taps on his pad and the ship's weapons unfolded from the hull over the heads of two now thoroughly persuaded bureaucrats. The [[security-officer]] did not get the message, and he and [[rethven]] tumbled through a doorway into a corridor and out of everyone's sight. [[carsk]] arrived, got the man into a hold, and [[rethven]] finished it by loosening his own breath mask and exhaling [[ammonia]] into the man's face until he went limp.

He is alive. Everyone checked twice.

## Thirty Percent

Back at the ramp with uniforms, [[security-cards]] and data pads in hand, [[kess]] worked on [[vessaly]] — no longer a hostage so much as a partner, once it emerged that he is exactly as corruptible as the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]] is shiny. A story about very secret cargo for a very particular client, a negotiation that started at ten percent and somehow finished at thirty, and [[vessaly]] was inside the plan.

He can find [[ilyra-dane]]. He also let slip two names nobody had before: [[casimir-aldovane]], a Nubian art dealer who comes here with her, and [[tessick]], who shadows them both and whom [[vessaly]] does not think is any kind of assistant.
