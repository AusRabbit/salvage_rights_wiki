The [[nubian-aqua-splendor]]'s security chief. [[vessaly]] warned that searching directly for [[ilyra-dane]] could attract his attention and described him as strict about access to guest and security data.

The crew avoided that scrutiny by using [[ottho]]'s unofficial terminal in the [[service-tunnels]] and the stolen credentials of [[michael]]. Severin has not yet appeared in person.

## Session VIII — The Warden

Severin has now appeared in person. He is styled as *Warden*, and he is described as imperious and very curious-looking. He wears no cape and is not a Sith. He walked into [[iresa-tarn-kavu|Iresa]]'s wrecked suite after the crew had gone, ran a hand over the blaster scoring, looked over the drone casings, gave the attendant who had failed at the door a few terse words and then checked the balcony.

His only order was: *find the Gand.* The only clue he has is the name [[carsk]] shouted on the way out.
