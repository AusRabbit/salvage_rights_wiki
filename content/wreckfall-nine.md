The crew came here after the sale aboard [[long-wake|the Long Wake]] to collect the four-person former Imperial quarantine team led by [[vossk]]. The group also included [[brak-tanris]], [[tavi-renn]] and [[emon-vale]].

Their briefing connected the [[specimen]] to the origin site and established the danger posed by its copied voices, personal knowledge and ability to exploit containment procedure. From Wreckfall Nine, the enlarged expedition continued toward [[the-dome]].
