The crew brought the witnesses and evidence from the [[harbingers-due|CSS Harbinger's Due]] here while both [[cygnus-biotech]] and [[verrin-kallax]] remained interested in what they carried. [[un-momento]] operated from Stall 19 in the Rain Market and directed them toward [[marda-voss]]'s hidden evidence vault.

Marda sealed an anomaly recording, creating an evidence trail outside corporate control. The crew captured [[pavo]], fought [[drenn]] and coerced [[talla-breen]] into releasing the ship's docking clamps. Pavo died, Drenn was left alive but badly wounded, and Talla remained free. Service surveillance captured enough of the violence to be dangerous. The crew escaped aboard the Wayfarer's Debt, but the forensic and administrative consequences were never shown to be settled.

A later unidentified vessel used a Port Heska transponder shell while approaching [[long-wake|the Long Wake]]. Its owner remains unknown.
