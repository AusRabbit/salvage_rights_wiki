The crew brought the witnesses and evidence from the [[harbingers-due|CSS Harbinger's Due]] here while both [[cygnus-biotech]] and [[verrin-kallax]] remained interested in what they carried.

[[marda-voss]] sealed an anomaly recording, creating an evidence trail outside corporate control. A violent confrontation disrupted Kallax's local people and was captured by service surveillance. The crew escaped aboard the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]], but the forensic and administrative consequences were never shown to be settled.

A later unidentified vessel used a Port Heska transponder shell while approaching [[long-wake|the Long Wake]]. Its owner remains unknown.
