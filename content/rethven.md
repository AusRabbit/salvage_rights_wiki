Spent a decade inside [[the-dome]] and has been almost nowhere since, which shows: he bounds down the ramp at the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]] purely because it is somewhere new, and gets mistaken for a reclusive billionaire on the strength of not understanding what credits are.

Breathes [[ammonia]] through a mask, which became a weapon when a glancing blow knocked it loose mid-scrap — he held his breath, sat on the [[security-officer]]'s chest and exhaled until the man stopped moving. Awkward, effective, and entirely in character.

His obligation is a fascination with what cannot be explained. It triggered on the closing roll, so the retreat is about to become interesting for him specifically.
