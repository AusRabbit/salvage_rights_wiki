Spent a decade inside [[the-dome]] and has been almost nowhere since, which shows: he bounds down the ramp at the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]] purely because it is somewhere new, and gets mistaken for a reclusive billionaire on the strength of not understanding what credits are.

Breathes [[ammonia]] through a mask, which became a weapon when a glancing blow knocked it loose mid-scrap — he held his breath, sat on the [[security-officer]]'s chest and exhaled until the man stopped moving. Awkward, effective, and entirely in character.

His obligation is a fascination with what cannot be explained.

## At the Splendor

The desk accepted Rethven as the party's principal guest, and the crew followed that misunderstanding into an eighth-floor penthouse. He threw himself into rooftop Pilates, then later noticed maintenance workers moving around the balconies near [[ilyra-dane]]'s suite. Those exterior routes may offer a way in.

When [[kess]] accused Gungan room service of stealing his wife's Nubian pearl necklace, Rethven played the distressed wife convincingly enough to help draw [[trent]] away from [[michael]].

## Session VII — The Door

Rethven kept watch in the corridor beside a hijacked room-service trolley, pocketing the [[corellian-whiskey]] tasters, and greeted [[iresa-tarn-kavu|Iresa]] with a loud offer of whiskey. He heard the drones first. When the trolley proved useless against things that fly, he ducked into the suite and held the door shut with his back. His Pilates training — leverage and the right foothold rather than strength — held off the [[splendor-security|security attendants]] for most of the fight.

He shouted “Hostage!” at exactly the wrong person and drove a flight of drones back into the corridor. On the balcony, he bagged [[captured-skylark-drone|the last Skylark]] and punched it quiet.

[[carsk]] called him “Gann” in front of security. The obligation roll came up Rethven for next session.
