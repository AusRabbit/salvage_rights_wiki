The crew began where the previous session ended: in the galley of the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]], with one unconscious security officer, a terrified attendant and a very profitable understanding with [[vessaly]]. Vessaly left [[sean]] to watch the prisoner and led [[carsk]], [[kess]] and [[rethven]] into the hidden workings of the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]]. [[sel]] came along; [[rhett]] stayed aboard with the sand.

## Through the Bowels

The way up ran through stairs, access corridors and the machinery that turns the resort's waterfalls into power. Before the public levels, Vessaly stopped the crew at the weapons line. Rifles and carbines went into a secured cupboard bought with a destiny point; pistols, a vibroknife and Carsk's knucklers remained concealed.

The Splendor above was almost offensively beautiful: greenery, mountain views, polished corridors and a many-storey waterfall descending through its central atrium. Vessaly installed the group in a deluxe penthouse on the eighth floor, with [[rethven]] presented as the wealthy guest and “Mr Shubidua” and “Lenny” entered as two of his associates. The price of Vessaly's help climbed to fifty percent.

## The Penthouse Stakeout

The crew briefly committed to their cover with alarming enthusiasm. Rethven and Kess attended rooftop Pilates; Kess followed it with enough treatments to emerge glowing. Sel sampled the spa and fell asleep in the penthouse. Carsk stayed behind with the top-shelf brandy, a bath and the balcony.

That made him the first to spot [[ilyra-dane]] several rooms away. She was in a robe, sharing drinks and affection with a portly, self-assured man presumed to be [[casimir-aldovane]]. Carsk hid successfully and fixed the location of her suite.

When Casimir returned to the balcony, he was joined by a comms-wearing figure in a bulky jacket, presumed to be [[tessick]]. Carsk made out the shape of a terse, conspiratorial business conversation. It stopped the instant Dane appeared. The three withdrew inside, but not before the bodyguard glanced towards the crew's balcony. Rethven noticed maintenance workers using the exterior routes around the suites — another possible way in.

## Michael Walks

Vessaly's message changed the priority. His associate had failed to hold the security officer, whose name was now established as [[michael]]. Michael had woken, left the ship and was moving through the resort. Vessaly surrendered five percent for the mistake, leaving his cut at forty-five.

Vessaly introduced the crew to [[ottho]], the Gungan chief of maintenance, at the Level 3 sun deck. Ottho led them into a network of broad [[service-tunnels]] and gave them a secluded terminal. Using Michael's stolen [[security-cards|security ID]], Kess accessed the staff cameras, found him near the grand staircase and plotted a direct route through the tunnels.

Carsk ran. He burst back into the atrium, surfed down the lobby waterfall and intercepted Michael on the stairs. A concealed vibroknife and a successful threat kept the officer still, but his colleague [[trent]] arrived and called out to him.

Kess turned the resort's prejudice into a distraction. Drawing on his knowledge of Naboo, he claimed that Gungan room service had stolen his wife's Nubian pearl necklace; Rethven played the distraught wife. Trent accepted the story and went to deal with it. Michael, pleading that his sick father and unemployed mother depend on his wage, agreed to punch out and spend the next twenty-four hours in the crew's penthouse. Carsk escorted him away without bloodshed.

## A Face from the Board

The session closed on Carsk's obligation. On the way downstairs with Michael, he passed a diplomat whose face he recognised from the web of photographs and red string aboard the Wayfarer's Debt — part of his long investigation into the terrorist attack that killed his family.

The diplomat remains unnamed. The recognition does not.

Each player received 10 XP.
