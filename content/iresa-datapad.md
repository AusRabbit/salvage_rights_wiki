[[iresa-tarn-kavu|Iresa Tarn-Kavu]]'s work pad. [[carsk]] found it neatly packed in her luggage while tearing her suite apart, before she returned from her treatment.

As the crew escaped, Carsk tossed it to [[kess]], who knows how to get data out of devices like this one. Kess carried it down the grapnel line.

## Status

Held by Kess. **Not yet read.** Whatever a diplomat carries on her personal pad — correspondence, schedules, contacts, or something about the shuttle that took Carsk's family off-world — is still locked inside.
