Broad passages, maintenance hatches and access corridors threaded throughout the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]]. They connect the deep machinery levels to the guest floors and allow staff to move without crossing the resort's polished public spaces.

[[ottho]] gave the crew access and led them to an unofficial terminal connected to staff cameras. From there, [[kess]] used [[michael]]'s security ID and the resort's facial recognition to locate him and map a fast route to the grand staircase.

The network also reaches the exterior maintenance routes around [[ilyra-dane]]'s suite, making it a possible approach for the next operation.
