Flying security drones about the size of a dinner plate. Earlier in the stay, the crew noticed drone dispensaries throughout the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]], and the drones turned out to be the resort's quick-reaction force. They answered [[iresa-tarn-kavu]]'s panic button well ahead of the human guards.

## Kess's Briefing

[[kess]] recognised the buzzing before anyone saw the drones and briefed the crew mid-fight:

- **Small and fast.** They swarm, fly in loose flights and reach awkward places quickly.
- **Stun emitter, with a lethal mode.** Built-in, short-ranged; they have to close in before they fire.
- **Easily entangled.** Rope, towels, awnings: anything that wraps them up stops them.
- **They float.** Barricades on the floor do not slow them, and a balcony is just another entrance.
- **They use the walls.** Hatches and chutes let them move through the building and appear inside a sealed room.

The briefing paid off: the crew's first shots against them came easier than they should have.

## The Encounter

Two flights came down the corridor outside Iresa's suite, with two [[splendor-security|security attendants]] behind them.

- Rethven's trolley barricade did nothing; they flew over it.
- Kess cut power to the room's hatches by pulling wires from the wall.
- The splintering door kept knocking drones aside while Rethven held it.
- Kess shot one out of the air from cover in the spa.
- Rethven's shot drove a whole flight back into the corridor.
- One drone flashed as it swept the room — **apparently recording the crew**.
- Carsk hit the power core of a drone in the middle of the pack; the explosion took two more with it.
- The last drone tried to escape onto the balcony with its recording, got tangled in a privacy awning and was [[captured-skylark-drone|captured by Rethven]].

**Tally:** four destroyed, one captured. The second flight was still active when the crew went over the balcony.
