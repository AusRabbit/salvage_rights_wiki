Reached the corridor door at the same moment [[rethven]] did and would not let go. Took a pin from [[carsk]], kept reaching for his baton anyway, and was finally put out by [[ammonia]]. His [[security-cards|security ID]] and uniform went to the crew.

His name became known when he woke aboard the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]], slipped past [[sean]] and returned to the resort. [[kess]] tracked him through staff cameras using the stolen ID. Carsk intercepted him on the grand staircase and held a concealed vibroknife to his ribs.

Michael pleaded that his sick father and unemployed mother depend on his income. He agreed to punch out and spend the next twenty-four hours in the crew's penthouse, and Carsk escorted him away. He has not changed sides; he is frightened, compromised and on a clock.
