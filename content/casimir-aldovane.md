Held in high esteem in Nubian political circles, and comes to the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]] with [[ilyra-dane]] periodically, for treatments and — per [[vessaly]] — other things.

[[carsk]] saw Dane canoodling on a balcony with a portly, balding and conspicuously self-assured man in a robe. Vessaly's description makes Casimir the likely identification, though the crew have not spoken to him.

The same man later held a terse business conversation with a bulky, comms-wearing figure presumed to be [[tessick]]. The exchange appeared conspiratorial and stopped abruptly when Dane stepped onto the balcony. That reaction suggests an agenda they are keeping from her.

## Session VIII — But a Moment, Darling

The resort booked Casimir and Dane together for a 12:30 treatment in [[treatment-levels|Treatment Room 6 Delta]]. The crew watched the two of them slip in together, canoodling, while Casimir threw a secretive glance back over his shoulder. A few minutes later he stepped out — *but a moment, darling, I'll be right back* — and walked off in a different direction.

Soon afterwards, the room was sealed and overheated from outside. Dane called for him throughout. He never answered. Nothing yet proves he was responsible, but he chose a very convenient moment to leave.
