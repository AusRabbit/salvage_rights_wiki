[[michael]] settled into his coerced day off in the crew's penthouse, ordering room service and staying put. In return he helped the crew piece together [[ilyra-dane]]'s schedule: skin treatments, relaxation packages, exercise on the open deck — she is everywhere and hard to pin down. The crew know which suite she occupies, but her ever-present bodyguard worries them, and he seems to hold quiet, conspiratorial conversations with her companion [[casimir-aldovane]]. It is a hunch, not yet proof.

Down in the ship, [[vossk-squad]] gave the crew the cold shoulder. They are still aboard, still eating the food and still complaining about the sand, but they want no part in what comes next.

None of that was on [[carsk]]'s mind.

## A Face from the Board

The diplomat Carsk recognised at the end of the last session was [[iresa-tarn-kavu|Iresa Tarn-Kavu]], a Togruta from his time in Imperial service. As Carsk tells it, she was meant to take care of his family. The last time he saw them, they were with her at a shuttle port. He went out on assignment; when he came back, his family were dead and Iresa was gone.

He came back to the penthouse unusually agitated and sober, and dropped his vibroknife while trying to leave. [[kess]] had seen that look before — the last time, Carsk came back covered in somebody else's blood — and pushed him for the truth. Carsk gave it, and [[sel]] laid a hand on his shoulder and asked what he was going to do. When she turned away, out of sight of her mouth, she clicked.

Sel stayed behind with Michael. Kess and [[rethven]] went with Carsk.

## Room Service

A security swipe loaned by [[ottho]] opened the door to Iresa's suite in the middle of the afternoon. Kess went in first in a staff uniform from the resort laundry, announcing room service at every door. Rethven worked the corridor beside a hijacked room-service trolley, pocketing its [[corellian-whiskey]] tasters.

The suite was empty. Carsk could not wait for the all-clear and tore through it looking for her, smashing vases and knocking things flying. He did find what mattered: [[iresa-datapad|Iresa's work pad]], neatly packed in her luggage.

Iresa came back early from a treatment, politely declined Rethven's loudly offered whiskey and walked into her suite to find Kess. He explained that he and his colleague — whom he then named as Carsk — were checking the smoke alarms. The deception failed. Her hand went inside her robe, and she asked Kess to show her exactly where the integrated smoke detectors were. He could not.

Carsk had hidden. When he stepped out behind her, she turned, took a moment to steady herself and sat down. “Hello, Carsk.”

## The Panic Button

Iresa set a small handheld device on the coffee table. Carsk knew it at once: a diplomat's panic button, already pressed. Security was coming.

Carsk demanded answers and slammed his fists on the table. Kess pointed out that, since she had already called the guards, she had nothing left to threaten them with. Iresa did not flinch; she held the room as if she were back in the Senate. Then Carsk said his family were dead, and her lekku flicked.

“What makes you think they're dead?”

She told him they had boarded that shuttle and escaped the planet before the Imperial net closed. His family may be alive, though it has been some time since she saw them. It was the answer Carsk had wanted for years, from someone with every reason to say whatever would keep her alive.

## The Skylarks

Out in the corridor, Rethven heard a growing buzz. Kess identified it as [[skylark-security-drone|Skylark security drones]], the Splendor's quick-reaction force, and briefed the others: plate-sized, fast, swarming, armed with stun emitters that have a lethal setting, and easily tangled. Two flights of drones came round the corner, with two [[splendor-security|security attendants]] running behind them.

Rethven abandoned the trolley, ducked into the suite and slammed the door. With his back against it and a foot braced on the wall, his Pilates training held it shut against the guards outside. Kess pulled a handful of wires out of the wall to kill the drone hatches. The guards still forced the antique Kashyyyk-oak door off its hinges, but not open.

Drones slipped through the gap. Kess dived into the suite's spa for cover and shot one down. Rethven shouted “Hostage!” — an idea Iresa found very funny — and drove a flight back into the corridor. One drone began flashing as it swept the room, apparently recording them. Carsk finally turned his attention from Iresa and put a single shot into the power core of the drone in the middle of the pack. The blast took two more with it.

## A Flawless Exit

Kess grabbed Iresa's spent panic button and used *Mad Inventor* to build a signal jammer from wire, foil and a spare power cell, then threw it like a grenade. Iresa caught it and tossed it away. The device misfired spectacularly anyway: the lights died, the caf machine exploded and the blinds went haywire. A balcony privacy awning swept down and tangled the last drone just as it tried to fly off with whatever it had recorded.

The guards forced the door in. Carsk laid down stun fire to cover the retreat, tossed Iresa's work pad to Kess and shouted for “Gann” to follow him to the balcony. Rethven grabbed the tangled drone, took a few sparks for his trouble and punched it until it stopped moving.

Kess jumped backwards off the balcony and fired the grapnel built into his heavy blaster pistol in mid-air. It held. Iresa watched from the bar and waved.

## Close-out

The crew escaped apparently unseen, but somebody fired a blaster inside the Splendor, and security will not let that go. The obligation roll triggers [[rethven]] next session. Each player received 10 XP.
