The last surviving drone of the flight that broke into [[iresa-tarn-kavu]]'s suite. It fled onto the balcony and got tangled in a privacy awning that [[kess]]'s misfiring jammer had brought down. [[rethven]] grabbed it, took a few sparks for his trouble and punched it until it stopped struggling.

The crew escaped with it.

## Why It Matters

Before it fled, this drone was flashing as it swept the room. It appeared to be recording, and it tried to leave with whatever it had captured. If it holds images of the crew, they now have them and the Splendor does not. They also have a working example of the resort's security hardware.

What it actually recorded, and whether it can still report back to the Splendor, are both unknown.
