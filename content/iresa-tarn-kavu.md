A Togruta diplomat whom [[carsk]] knew during his time in Imperial service, and one of the faces on the board of red string aboard the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]]. By Carsk's account, she was meant to take care of his family. He last saw them with her at a shuttle port before he left on assignment. When he returned, his family were dead and Iresa was gone.

She was staying at the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]] under her own name, in a large suite near the crew's, filling her days with treatments.

## The Confrontation

She caught [[kess]] in her suite posing as a smoke-alarm inspector, saw through the act at once and kept her hand inside her robe while she let him fail to point out the detectors. She greeted Carsk by name, sat down and laid her panic button on the coffee table, already pressed.

She was unmoved by threats and remarkably calm under fire. She held the room as if she were back in the Senate, laughed at the idea that the crew could take her hostage, strolled to the bar while blaster bolts crossed the suite, and caught Kess's improvised jammer one-handed before tossing it away.

Her account contradicts Carsk's. She says she and his family boarded that shuttle and escaped the planet before the Imperial net closed, and that they may still be alive, though it has been some time since she saw them. She was also talking for her life at the time. Nothing she said has been verified.

She watched the crew go over the balcony and waved.

## What She Lost

Carsk took her [[iresa-datapad|work pad]] from her luggage before she returned. She may not know it is gone yet.

## The Panic Button

The device looks like a dead man's switch, but it is simply a handle with a single button: a diplomat's panic button that summons help fast. Pressing it brought the [[skylark-security-drone|Skylark drones]] and [[splendor-security]] to her door. Kess later rebuilt it into an improvised jammer, which failed in a way that wrecked her suite and helped the crew escape.
