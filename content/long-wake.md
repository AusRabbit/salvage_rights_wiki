A vast scrap barge moving through Ord Mantell's lower atmosphere beneath rain, floodlights and magnetic cranes. The crew chose it as neutral ground for the sale of material recovered from the [[harbingers-due|CSS Harbinger's Due]].

Here [[ilyra-dane]] identified the organism as [[specimen|MYRMEX-9]], bought the severed limb and cryogenic equipment, and gave the crew the coordinates that eventually led back to [[the-dome]].

An unidentified utility vessel approached during the sale using a [[port-heska]] transponder shell. Dane's personnel and the barge's machinery covered the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] as it escaped.
