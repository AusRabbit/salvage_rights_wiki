Flies the ship, fixes the ship, and is visibly happier doing the second. Spent most of this session with a toothbrush in the cockpit trying to get dome sand out from under the buttons, and delivered some of his best work of the campaign while only half listening.

His charm is geographically limited. Aboard the [[wayfarers-debt]] his social checks are upgraded and he is smooth, hospitable and quick — pouring [[corellian-whiskey]], talking traffic control in circles, promising a bureaucrat thirty percent of a cargo that does not exist. Off the ship, by his own cheerful admission, he is nothing special.

Carries a tier-four *Mad Inventor* talent: once per session he can attempt to cobble together the functional equivalent of any item out of salvage. He first used it in Session VII. He opened the evening carrying a critical injury from the dome, and closed it running a hostage situation like a dinner party.

At [[port-heska]], Kess kept [[rhett]] alive and the specimen limb cold through remote engineering instructions, then interfered with [[talla-breen]]'s access to the berth cameras. He later carried Rhett's anomaly recording into [[marda-voss]]'s vault and established the campaign's surviving evidence trail.

## At the Splendor

Kess concealed his pistol among the tools in his vest, attended rooftop Pilates and then committed fully to the resort's treatments. The resulting bill is enormous and, in his mind, somebody else's problem.

At [[ottho]]'s terminal, he used [[michael]]'s stolen security ID to search the resort's facial-recognition cameras and map a route through the [[service-tunnels]]. When [[trent]] threatened to expose Carsk's hold on Michael, Kess used *Applied Research* to substitute his knowledge of the Core Worlds for deception. His invented story about a stolen Nubian pearl necklace exploited the resort's prejudice against Gungans and drew Trent away.

## Session VII — Room Service

Kess went first into [[iresa-tarn-kavu|Iresa]]'s suite in a laundry-room staff uniform. When she came back, his smoke-alarm inspection story fell apart, not least because he said [[carsk]]'s name in front of her.

He made up for it once the fight began. He identified the [[skylark-security-drone|Skylark security drones]] by sound and briefed the crew, pulled wires to kill the room's drone hatches, then shot a drone down from cover inside the suite's spa. With *Mad Inventor*, he turned Iresa's spent panic button into a jammer. It failed, but it shut down the suite badly enough to tangle the last drone and cover the exit.

He left by jumping backwards off the balcony and firing the grapnel on his heavy blaster pistol in mid-air. He is now holding [[iresa-datapad|Iresa's datapad]], unread.
