Destination of the hyperspace jump [[kess]] quietly set while claiming he could not redirect. The [[nubian-aqua-splendor]] sits in its mountains, well above the swamps.

The crew's first day inside the resort exposed the divide beneath its polished Nubian surface: Gungan workers maintain the hidden infrastructure, while security officers such as [[trent]] treat suspicion of them as routine.
