The crew of the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] escaped [[the-dome]] after making a bargain with the [[specimen]]: recover the components taken by [[ilyra-dane]], and the creature will grant them access beneath the dome. The surviving members of [[vossk-squad]] revealed where Dane could be reached off the books — the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]] on [[naboo]].

## Current Position

The crew are installed in an eighth-floor penthouse under improvised guest identities. They have located Dane's suite, mapped service routes through the resort, retained covert access to its security network, and neutralised [[michael]] for roughly twenty-four hours. Their weapons remain hidden in a secure cupboard below the public levels.

Their local fixer [[vessaly]] now expects forty-five percent. [[ottho]], the Gungan chief of maintenance, has shown them passages that bypass the guest corridors. Dane has not yet been approached.

## Immediate Pressure

- [[casimir-aldovane]] and the presumed [[tessick]] cut short a conspiratorial conversation when Dane appeared.
- The presumed Tessick may have noticed the crew watching from their balcony.
- [[carsk]] recognised an unnamed diplomat as a face from his investigation into the attack that killed his family.
- Michael's coerced day off creates a clock, not a permanent solution.
