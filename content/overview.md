The crew of the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] escaped [[the-dome]] after making a bargain with the [[specimen]]: recover the components taken by [[ilyra-dane]], and the creature will grant them access beneath the dome. The surviving members of [[vossk-squad]] revealed where Dane could be reached off the books — the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]] on [[naboo]].

## Current Position

The crew are installed in an eighth-floor penthouse under improvised guest identities. They know [[ilyra-dane]]'s suite and treatment schedule, can move through the service routes, and retain covert access to the resort's security network. [[michael]] is spending his coerced day off in the penthouse. Their weapons remain hidden in a secure cupboard below the public levels, and [[vossk-squad]] are still sulking aboard the ship.

Session VII was [[carsk]]'s. The diplomat from his board is [[iresa-tarn-kavu|Iresa Tarn-Kavu]]. When confronted, she claimed his family escaped with her and may be alive. Her panic button brought [[skylark-security-drone|Skylark drones]] and [[splendor-security]] down on her suite, and the crew escaped over the balcony with [[iresa-datapad|her datapad]] and [[captured-skylark-drone|a captured drone]].

Session VIII went back to the main mission. The crew stole [[ilyra-dane]]'s 12:30 appointment on the [[treatment-levels|treatment levels]] and got into Treatment Room 6 Delta with her. Then someone else sealed the room and tried to cook everyone inside. [[kess]] sliced them out against active opposition. Dane, wounded and abandoned by [[casimir-aldovane]], came out with them and asked: “What now?”

## Immediate Pressure

- Dane is face to face with the crew, armed, hurt and newly suspicious of her own people.
- Someone at the Splendor tried to kill her, and nearly killed the crew too. Casimir and the presumed [[tessick]] are the obvious suspects.
- [[severin|Warden Severin]] is hunting “the Gand” after the shooting in Iresa's suite, and a Gungan matriarch has raised the alarm on the treatment levels.
- Iresa's datapad is still unread, and the captured drone may hold images of the crew.
- [[michael]]'s day off is a clock, not a permanent solution.
- [[kess]]'s obligation, the ship's murky ownership, has triggered but may be held back.
