A bulk freighter found drifting in the Gorrath asteroid belt with power and life support still running but almost no crew aboard. The [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] reached it as [[verrin-kallax]]'s salvagers and [[cygnus-biotech]] converged on the same claim.

Inside, [[kess]] and [[carsk]] found a failed containment operation and an organism loose in the vessel's structure. They escaped with [[rhett]], a severed specimen limb, cryogenic equipment and evidence that Cygnus regarded the ship as a recovery site rather than a rescue.

The precise room-to-room history is intentionally omitted from the public reconstruction. What endured was the lesson that the [[specimen]] can exploit powered infrastructure and that abrupt cold can slow it.
