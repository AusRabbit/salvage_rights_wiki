Kept [[vossk]] as part of a bargain and let the rest of them go. In exchange for access to whatever lies beneath [[the-dome]], it asked the crew to recover its missing components from [[ilyra-dane]] — components the crew themselves cut off and sold.

It had tendrils in people and was controlling them, a detail [[kess]] found reassuring on the grounds that it clearly knows its way around a body.

*Table note: saying its name is a running bit with a secret clock attached. The transcript contains roughly thirty consecutive invocations. This is a genuinely hard case for a summariser, which will read the repetition as significance.*
