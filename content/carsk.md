A veteran of a real war, which is the credential that got him through the door with [[vossk-squad]] when nothing else would have. Covered in fur and, by the end of sixteen whiskies, the most respected person in the room.

He works in two registers and switches between them without warning. In the first he is warm to the point of being disarming — a hand on [[brak-tanris]]'s shoulder, sincere condolences about [[vossk]], a song on the dining table. In the second he has a man against a bulkhead with [[vibro-knucklers]] on, offering him the choice of conscious or unconscious, and is startled to find he has gone too far.

Holds a talent that lets him call in a small favour from current or former military personnel, and another that supports an ally's check simply by looking like a problem. Both saw use this session.

## At the Splendor

Carsk used the crew's eighth-floor balcony as a stakeout and was the first to spot [[ilyra-dane]]. He fixed the location of her suite, identified her companion as the likely [[casimir-aldovane]], and partially read a conspiratorial exchange between Casimir and the presumed [[tessick]]. The bodyguard may have noticed him watching.

When [[michael]] escaped the ship, Carsk sprinted through the [[service-tunnels]], surfed down the lobby waterfall and caught him on the grand staircase. He held a vibroknife discreetly to the officer's ribs, then talked him into taking a twenty-four-hour day off rather than hurting him.

## The Board

Carsk has spent years investigating the mastermind behind the terrorist attack that killed his family. Aboard the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]], faces and red string chart what he has learned. While escorting Michael away, he recognised a diplomat as one of those faces: [[iresa-tarn-kavu|Iresa Tarn-Kavu]], whom he knew from his Imperial service. By his account, she was meant to look after his family. He last saw them with her at a shuttle port; when he came back from assignment, they were dead and she was gone.

## Un Mo'mento

[[un-momento]] is an old Bothan contact and former romantic entanglement. Carsk once broke her refresher window, left without calling and disappeared while in a bad way. At [[port-heska]] he brought her whiskey and finally gave a specific apology. It did not earn forgiveness, but it did earn the lead that took the crew to [[marda-voss]].

## Session VII — Iresa

The obligation took over. Carsk broke into Iresa's suite with [[ottho]]'s loaned swipe, tore it apart looking for her and came away with [[iresa-datapad|her work pad]]. When she returned, he stepped out of hiding and demanded answers. She did not give much, but she did tell him his family may be alive.

Carsk lost his composure badly: dropped knives, smashed vases, fists on the table. He recovered only when the drones arrived. Then he made the shot of the night, hitting one [[skylark-security-drone|Skylark]]'s power core and taking out three drones at once. He covered the retreat with stun fire rather than shed organic blood, and threw the datapad to [[kess]] on the way out.

He still believes Rethven's name is “Gann”, and said so loudly enough for security to hear.
