A veteran of a real war, which is the credential that got him through the door with [[vossk-squad]] when nothing else would have. Covered in fur and, by the end of sixteen whiskies, the most respected person in the room.

He works in two registers and switches between them without warning. In the first he is warm to the point of being disarming — a hand on [[brak-tanris]]'s shoulder, sincere condolences about [[vossk]], a song on the dining table. In the second he has a man against a bulkhead with [[vibro-knucklers]] on, offering him the choice of conscious or unconscious, and is startled to find he has gone too far.

Holds a talent that lets him call in a small favour from current or former military personnel, and another that supports an ally's check simply by looking like a problem. Both saw use this session.

## At the Splendor

Carsk used the crew's eighth-floor balcony as a stakeout and was the first to spot [[ilyra-dane]]. He fixed the location of her suite, identified her companion as the likely [[casimir-aldovane]], and partially read a conspiratorial exchange between Casimir and the presumed [[tessick]]. The bodyguard may have noticed him watching.

When [[michael]] escaped the ship, Carsk sprinted through the [[service-tunnels]], surfed down the lobby waterfall and caught him on the grand staircase. He held a vibroknife discreetly to the officer's ribs, then talked him into taking a twenty-four-hour day off rather than hurting him.

## The Board

Carsk has spent years investigating the mastermind behind the terrorist attack that killed his family. Aboard the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]], faces and red string chart what he has learned. While escorting Michael away, he recognised an unnamed diplomat as one of those faces. That lead is now inside the Splendor.
