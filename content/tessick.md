A very strange individual who is always with them. [[vessaly]] describes him as an assistant and then immediately says he thinks it is more than that.

The crew later watched a figure in a bulky jacket and communications headset arguing quietly with [[casimir-aldovane]]. The business relationship and bodyguard role fit Vessaly's description of Tessick, but the identification remains an in-character presumption. Their conversation stopped when [[ilyra-dane]] appeared. Before going inside, the figure glanced towards the crew's balcony and may have noticed the surveillance.

In Session VII, the crew noted that Dane's ever-present bodyguard keeps holding quiet, conspiratorial conversations with [[casimir-aldovane]].

## Session VIII — The Sauna

Dane's suspicious follower walked into the treatment-level sauna where [[carsk]] was sitting, put his feet up and sprawled. The crew are presuming this is Tessick, but his name was not used at the table. Carsk drove him out with a fart. Before leaving, the man looked straight at Carsk, narrowed his eyes and asked: *Was it you on the balcony a day ago?* He has connected Carsk to the surveillance.

Later, as [[ilyra-dane]] and [[casimir-aldovane]] entered [[treatment-levels|6 Delta]], a figure moved sideways through another passage and away. Carsk thought it looked a lot like the same man. Shortly afterwards the room was locked and overheated from outside.
