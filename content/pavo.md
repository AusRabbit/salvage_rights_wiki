A Rodian paid by [[verrin-kallax]] to watch for the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] at [[port-heska]]. His team were checking fuel stops, medical suppliers and underworld brokers for an ugly light hauler with fresh hull damage and wounded passengers.

The crew realised Pavo was following them and deliberately led him into the wrong service entrance near [[marda-voss]]'s vault. [[carsk]] broke his resistance with “Be smart or be dead.” Pavo revealed that Kallax wanted the crew found before [[cygnus-biotech]] reached them and preferred a grab to an open firefight. His compromised comm also exposed [[drenn]] and [[talla-breen]] near the ship.

Carsk later shot Pavo in the service corridor. Pavo was helpless at the time, which disturbed [[sel]] and made her more cautious around the crew. Port service cameras likely captured enough of the killing to remain a liability.
