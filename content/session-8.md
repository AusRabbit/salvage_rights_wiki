Back in [[iresa-tarn-kavu|Iresa]]'s wrecked suite, the man in charge arrived to look at the damage. [[severin|Warden Severin]] — no cape, not a Sith, and plainly not amused — ran a hand over the blaster scoring on the wall, looked at the drone casings scattered across the floor, had a few terse words with the attendant who had failed to get through the door, then leaned over the balcony. The crew were long gone. He gave one order: *find the Gand.*

It is the only lead security has, and it helps the crew more than it hurts them. Thanks to [[carsk]], the Splendor is now hunting for a Gand named Gand.

The crew had their own loot to think about. [[iresa-datapad|Iresa's datapad]] stays dark for now: nobody is foolish enough to switch it on inside the resort with dead man's switches and bit-lockers to worry about. [[captured-skylark-drone|The captured drone]] is stuffed in a sock. With Carsk's detour behind them, it was back to the mission: [[ilyra-dane]].

## Towels Only

Carsk, [[kess]] and [[rethven]], wearing nothing but towels, stepped through the double doors into the treatment levels of the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]], with [[sel]] alongside. Beyond the doors lay a warm, heavy, damp warren of corridors and treatment rooms, staffed almost entirely by Gungans. A young Gungan receptionist assured them they were definitely not in a sewer.

Carsk dropped his towel and asked for the sauna. She covered her eyes, pointed and sent him on his way.

That left the others to explain themselves. Kess introduced Rethven as a Geonosian — a rather fat one, which is why he looks so much like a Gand, and why he needs Pilates. His wings were tucked into his towel. The receptionist believed every word and typed it into the guest record. Rethven then put on the air of a guest who should not need to be asked, and claimed the 12:30 appointment.

She checked. The 12:30 was booked for Ilyra Dane and [[casimir-aldovane]], in Treatment Room 6 Delta. She handed over the treatment slip with shaking hands and asked them to go away. Kess and Rethven collected Carsk from the sauna and gave him the news: he was Casimir now.

## The Man in the Sauna

While they talked, another man in a towel came in, sat down and put his feet up. Carsk recognised him: not Casimir, but the suspicious follower who shadows Dane, presumably [[tessick]].

Carsk wanted the room to themselves. His solution was a protein-bar diet's worth of methane, released beside the vent with a wink to his friends. The man coughed, wrinkled his nose and looked straight at Carsk. *Was it you on the balcony a day ago?* His eyes narrowed and he left.

The fart did more damage to the crew than to him. Kess, who has shared a ship with Carsk for a long time, held his breath and mostly got away with it. Rethven did not. The smell hit him so hard that he fled the room in a blind panic, lost his towel and ended up naked in a corridor, surrounded by young Gungan attendants trying to talk him down. When he recovered, he told them his therapist had recommended exploratory social engagements, and asked politely about his 12:30.

An older Gungan matriarch with gnarled masseuse's fingers was not fooled. She slapped the receptionist for letting them through and told Rethven to stay right where he was. Carsk and Kess arrived to tell her someone had made an awful mess in the sauna — not just a smell, Kess insisted, it was in the jets — and needed cleaning up. She had recognised them as the people who assaulted the Togruta envoy earlier, but she was not going to stand in a corridor with a wild Gand and a smelly Bothan. She bowed, said this was oddly not the first time it had happened, and walked away. Once she was out of sight, her pace quickened. In the distance, they heard her calling for help.

## Bad Cosplay

On her abandoned desk the crew found a facility schematic on a clipboard, along with the key to a nearby supply room. Kess's plan was simple: stop looking like thugs in towels and start looking like patrons. Seaweed masks, hair wraps, foils, highlights in Carsk's fur and a pair of coat hangers bent into wings for the Gand. It was bad cosplay, but they committed to it.

The route to 6 Delta ran along a passage beside a warm geothermal stream. Staff on an induction tour explained that the water is the whole point: tapped off the mountain, mineral-rich and piped into every treatment room. Two of the three disguises held. Kess's seaweed kept sliding off his oily face.

Ahead, they watched Dane and Casimir slip into 6 Delta together. Casimir threw a secretive glance back over his shoulder. Down another passage, a figure moved sideways and out of sight. To Carsk, it looked a lot like the man from the sauna.

The crew waited a few minutes. Then Casimir reappeared in the doorway — *but a moment, darling, I'll be right back* — and walked off in another direction.

## Treatment Room 6 Delta

Rethven stayed outside as lookout and Sel took up a post in the corridor. Carsk and Kess went in.

Dane lay face down beneath a towel on one of two beds. The geothermal water wound under the wall, into basins and little cascades, and out the far side. She murmured an invitation to join her. Kess put on a fluffy hotel robe and hood, took her hand and said nothing. When she asked what his favourite thing about yesterday had been, he answered with a knowing chuckle. Carsk waved Rethven in, and Rethven promptly forgot the plan. He had lived for a decade in [[the-dome]], and this was the most beautiful water he had ever seen. He knelt at the stream and put his face in it.

A Gungan masseuse arrived. Carsk introduced himself as the intern on his first day and was put to work. Then the room began to heat up.

The masseuse told Carsk to cool it down. The wall panel threw errors at everything he pressed. Kess slipped over to check it and confirmed what was happening: the panel was dead, and the fault was outside the room. Carsk sent the masseuse for more Nubian massage oil. She walked face-first into the door. It was locked.

The heat climbed again. Kess was badly winded. Dane was hurt too, and when she lifted her head she saw a Gungan, a skinny human, a poofy Bothan and someone bobbing for apples in the stream. She panicked, flattened herself against the scalding wall and produced a holdout blaster.

## The Enemy of My Enemy

Carsk put his hands up. *Casimir isn't here. He left before any of this started. If we wanted to hurt you, we wouldn't be locked in here with you.* Dane wanted to know who had been holding her hand. Carsk turned, without meaning to, and looked straight at Kess.

Kess turned round empty-handed and told her the truth: they had come to talk, and whatever was happening to the room was somebody else's work. The masseuse sobbed in the corner. The second door, behind a curtain, had a keypad too, and it was hot enough to scorch a hand. Kess pulled the panel off and started on the wiring. Dane lowered her pistol, struggling for breath.

Carsk whispered to Rethven: *go with the water.* Rethven lost his footing, the current took him, and he was sucked out of the room sideways. Carsk threw Kess a soaked towel. It landed on his head and fouled his work at the panel. Then Carsk shouted through the door to Sel, who went to look for Rethven.

Rethven surfaced in a cool, quiet passage, shook himself off — still no towel — and found his way back after a few wrong turns. The corridors were empty. That was not how they had looked on the way in.

## Out of the Oven

Kess got the door open on the second attempt, using the security keys [[ottho]] had given him, with the air conditioning coming on behind him. It should have been a straightforward bypass. Instead, every input was slowed and every route through the system twisted back on itself. Someone else had been in the system, working against him in real time.

The door opened onto cool corridor air. Dane came out after them, wounded and deflated. She had called for Casimir throughout. He never answered. As she began to understand what that might mean, she started to cry. Then she looked up at the crew.

“What now?”

Kess told her they had come for a stern conversation, not to cook her alive, and that they were not her worst enemies in this place.

## Close-out

Each player received 10 XP. The obligation roll came up [[kess]]. Asked to explain his debt, Kess admitted, with Carsk listening, that he did not win the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] in a legendary game of cards. He bought her from a shonky dealer, disabled the tracking so she could not be repossessed, and left. Possession is nine-tenths of the law, as long as the ship stays ahead of the debtors. The GM may hold that obligation for a few sessions.
