A human slicer working with [[pavo]] and [[drenn]] in [[verrin-kallax]]'s local finder team. Talla used port-maintenance access to reach berth cameras and lock the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] into its docking clamps.

She detected that Pavo had been compromised and moved against the ship while [[rhett]] was alone aboard. [[kess]] interfered with her camera access; Rhett sealed the hatch and exposed her position with a maintenance floodlight.

At the berth, [[carsk]] coerced her into surrendering her blaster and releasing the clamp lockout by using Drenn's survival as leverage. Kess took her port-service slicer tool and its temporary maintenance credentials.

Talla was alive when the crew launched and wanted to return to Drenn. Her later actions are unknown.
