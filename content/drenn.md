A Nikto member of [[verrin-kallax]]'s local finder team. While [[pavo]] followed the crew through the Rain Market, Drenn watched the fuel stacks and the berth of the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]].

Carsk fought him in the service corridor, took a blaster wound and incapacitated him with disciplined rifle fire. Drenn was alive when the crew left, but badly wounded and unattended. Whether [[talla-breen]] reached him or Port Heska services found him was never established.

Carsk took Drenn's comm and an encrypted bounty puck containing partial contract and claim information tied to Kallax's operation. Neither source had been fully exploited in the surviving ledgers.
