An engineer rescued from the [[harbingers-due|CSS Harbinger's Due]] and a practical witness to the [[specimen]]'s shipboard behaviour. His technical evidence helped [[marda-voss]] create a sealed anomaly record at [[port-heska]]. That history also makes him someone [[cygnus-biotech]] may wish to control.

Later responsible for putting the [[wayfarers-debt]] into the dirt, and currently slinking around it feeling poorly about himself. [[kess]] is in no hurry to let him leave — there is a debt to work off and nowhere to go.

He remained aboard during the crew's first day inside the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]], sweeping out sand and being kept away from the ship's unlabelled controls.
