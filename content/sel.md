A former [[cygnus-biotech]] insider who joined the crew after the [[harbingers-due|Harbinger]] crisis. She helped interpret the organism and warned that Cygnus would treat witnesses as a containment problem. [[ilyra-dane]] recognised her during the sale aboard [[long-wake|the Long Wake]], but Sel did not explain their complete history.

Wanted a gentler outcome at [[the-dome]] than the one she got, and is still sour about it. She is also making a clicking noise in the ship's corridors late at night, which [[kess]] has not stopped thinking about.

She assisted on the medicine check that closed [[kess]]'s critical injury, and proved a font of knowledge she has never demonstrated before. The open question is whether she always had it and hid it, or whether it arrived recently along with everything else.

[[carsk]] has stated in front of her that he intends to get whatever is in her body out of it. She asked what he was talking about. Nobody answered.

At the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]], Sel passed easily among the guests, sampled the treatments and became relaxed enough to fall asleep in the crew's penthouse. The clicking and unexplained medical knowledge remain unresolved.
