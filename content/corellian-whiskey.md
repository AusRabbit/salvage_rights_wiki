Produced from a sand-jammed drinks machine by percussive maintenance, and responsible for turning three hostile Imperials into crew. Sixteen of them went into [[carsk]]. Later offered to prisoners as a courtesy.

At the Splendor, [[rethven]] lifted the Corellian whiskey tasters from a room-service trolley and offered one, loudly, to [[iresa-tarn-kavu|Iresa Tarn-Kavu]]. She declined.
