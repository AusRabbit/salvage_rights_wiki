The salvage organisation led by [[verrin-kallax]]. It first competed with the crew for the [[harbingers-due|CSS Harbinger's Due]], initially accepting a joint claim before the specimen crisis and [[cygnus-biotech]] intervention broke the arrangement down.

At [[port-heska]], Kallax paid a local finder team to locate the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]] before Cygnus did. [[pavo]] provided surveillance, [[drenn]] covered the berth and fuel stacks, and [[talla-breen]] penetrated the port-service network. Their instructions favoured a controlled grab over open violence.

The operation ended with Pavo dead, Drenn incapacitated and Talla coerced into freeing the ship. Carsk left with Drenn's comm and encrypted bounty puck, giving the crew an unused route into the network's contracts and communications.
