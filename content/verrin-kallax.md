Led the competing salvage crew that reached the [[harbingers-due|CSS Harbinger's Due]] shortly after the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]]. He initially accepted a joint claim, but mutual suspicion and the arrival of [[cygnus-biotech]] turned the derelict into a three-sided struggle.

Kallax survived. His local network later moved against the crew at [[port-heska]], where the operation was badly disrupted. He still knows enough about the specimen, the witnesses and the sale to matter.

Whether his next move is retaliation, bargaining or the sale of information remains open.
