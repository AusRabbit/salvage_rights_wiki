Chief of maintenance at the [[nubian-aqua-splendor]], introduced by [[vessaly]] at the Level 3 sun-deck pool. Ottho is a dark-skinned Gungan equipped with the same sort of tools and practical clutter that [[kess]] carries.

He initially resisted giving strangers access to the security system, then accepted Vessaly's assurance that the problem was important. He opened the hidden [[service-tunnels]], led the crew to a secluded maintenance terminal and left them with access that [[severin]] was unlikely to notice.

Other Gungans work throughout the tunnels. What the crew saw suggests that the resort's immaculate public face depends on an underclass kept largely out of sight.
