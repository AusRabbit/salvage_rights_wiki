A sharp, stylish Bothan information broker based at Stall 19 in the Rain Market on [[port-heska]]. Her cream-and-smoke fur and single visible canine tooth make her easy to recognise. The story says the missing tooth was lost in a fight; the less romantic explanation is a dental problem.

Un specialises in introductions, blackmail handling, escrow contacts and salvage arbitration. She dislikes wasted time and can remain useful while openly angry.

## The Unpleasantness

Un and [[carsk]] were once romantically involved. She stopped trusting him after an episode remembered only as “the unpleasantness”: Carsk broke her refresher window, left without calling and was generally in a bad way.

When the crew needed help after the [[harbingers-due|Harbinger]] crisis, Carsk arrived with a three-quarter bottle of [[corellian-whiskey]] and an apology. Un kept the whiskey. She did not forgive him, but the specificity of the apology convinced her that the crew's danger was real.

## The Port Heska Lead

Un refused to broker a mysterious prize on vague promises. Instead, she sent the crew to [[marda-voss]], whom she trusted to classify dangerous evidence, establish custody and make corporate erasure expensive.

She also revealed that [[verrin-kallax]]'s money was behind the search for the [[wayfarers-debt|Wayfarer's Debt]], and identified his local finder team: [[pavo]], [[drenn]] and [[talla-breen]].

Her relationship with Carsk remained **angry but useful**. The final ledger placed their trust at three out of six.
