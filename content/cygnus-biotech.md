Sent a recovery force to the [[harbingers-due|CSS Harbinger's Due]] under quarantine authority. Its priority was the escaped research target and associated evidence, not the freighter's missing crew.

[[sel]] is a former Cygnus insider and believes the company moves quickly from asset recovery to witness control. The crew escaped with [[rhett]], specimen evidence and knowledge of the operation. Even after selling the physical limb to [[ilyra-dane]], they remain people Cygnus may wish to contain.

The ledgers consistently treat Cygnus as a continuing threat, but do not establish how much it presently knows about the crew's location.
